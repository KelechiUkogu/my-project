Indomie Stir-Fry Recipe

This quick and tasty stir-fry recipe is perfect for busy days when you want something delicious in minutes.

Ingredients

  • 1 pack of Indomie noodles
  • 1 tablespoon vegetable oil
  • ½ cup mixed vegetables (carrots, peas, bell peppers)
  • 1 boiled egg (optional)

Instructions

  1. Boil the noodles for 2–3 minutes and drain.
  2. Heat oil in a pan and sauté the vegetables.
  3. Add the noodles and seasoning mix, stir well.
  4. Top with boiled egg and serve hot.
