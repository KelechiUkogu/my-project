Step by step process for cooking jolllof rice

  1. Measure cups of the rice
  2. Bring out your pot for the cooking
  3. Add water into the pot
  4. Add the rice into the water
  5. Turn on the gascooker and place the pot on the gascooker
  6. Allow to cook
  7. Parboil after some minutes
  8. Add all neccessary seasoning into the pot
  9. Allow to cook until properly cooked
  10. Allow the water to dry and serve
